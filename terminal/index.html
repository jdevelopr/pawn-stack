      You run a pawn shop on Mars through a colony terminal. Every tool is a window. Drag them where you like.
      
      
        
        
      • AIRLOCK CAM: whoever is at your counter. Mornings bring sellers (offer, pay the ask, or pass). Afternoons bring buyers who want a category; pitch an item at a price.
        • 
        
      • SCANNER: spectro-scan the item on the counter (25 CR) to check for fakes and get a tight value range. Costs the seller 3s of patience. You can also scan your own stock.
        • 
        
      • DOSSIER: pull a customer's colony record (15 CR): their bottom line or budget, fraud flags, loan reliability. Costs 2s of patience.
        • 
        
      • STOCK.DB: your inventory and shelf prices. Walk-in shoppers buy anything priced below what they'd pay. At midday the doors close so you can set prices.
        • 
        
      • NETLINK: at midday or in the evening, breach a rival shop's books or intercept their sealed Pawn Guild answer (40 CR, 2 per sol). Security upgrades make you harder to breach. If you are traced you are fined and the target learns your name.
        • 
        
      • ADMIN: staff, upgrades and insurance, evenings only. LEDGER: standings, today's numbers, market news.
        • 
        
      • GUILD COMMS: every 5 sols the Pawn Guild posts a dilemma to every shop. Answer in secret. Results are anonymous, unless someone hacked you.
        • 
        
      • SHELL: type help. Everything can be done by command: offer 120, scan, pitch 3 400, hack kara books...
        • 
        
      • Rent, wages and insurance are paid each night. Run out of credits and stock and you are bankrupt.
