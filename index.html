      How to play

      • Mornings: sellers cycle through the airlock with goods. Offer low and haggle, pay their ask, or pass. Lowball too hard and they stop talking to you.
      • Afternoons: buyers ask for a category. Pitch an item and a price in credits (CR). Too high and they walk; close and they counter.
      • Your appraisal range is only a guess. Some items are fakes.
      • Evenings: pay dome rent and staff wages, then buy upgrades at the holo-board in the office.
      • Dump anything to the wholesaler for a fraction of its real value.
      • Customers are colonists, miners, Earth tourists, relic collectors, smugglers, spacers, belt prospectors, Grey brokers, Glorps and Zix. Each type haggles differently; the deal panel tells you who you are dealing with.
      • Multiplayer: everyone shares the same sellers and buyers. First to close the deal wins it. Haggling an ask down lowers it for rivals too.
      • A/D or arrows to walk, E to use the counter, shelves, or office. I opens inventory. Esc closes panels.
